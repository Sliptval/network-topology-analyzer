# Responsible use

nettop discovers hosts, probes ports and (optionally) captures traffic. Those
are ordinary network-administration activities on your own network, but on a
network you do not control they can be unwelcome or unlawful.

Please:

- **Only scan networks you own or have written permission to test.** Port
  scanning and packet capture against third-party networks may violate computer
  misuse laws and acceptable-use policies.
- **Be mindful of packet capture.** Capturing traffic can record sensitive data.
  Capture only what you need and store it securely.
- **Expect to need privileges.** OS fingerprinting and traffic capture require
  root/Administrator. The tool checks and tells you when it cannot proceed.

The authors provide this software for legitimate network management, education
and authorised security testing. You are responsible for how you use it.
